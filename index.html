Race paces (mile through marathon) are equivalent performances to the race you enter, using Daniels' race-equivalence tables. Threshold runs from halfway between 10K and half marathon pace out to half marathon pace. Steady is 8–13% slower than marathon pace, and easy is 20–32% slower.

A recent, all-out race on a flat course gives the best numbers. Heat, hills and wind push real paces slower. The example values are placeholders; replace them with your own.
