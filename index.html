How it works: the recent race sets today's fitness and the goal time sets the fitness needed on race day. Fitness is planned to improve evenly across the build, reaching goal level by the taper. Each phase trains at the fitness expected at its start, and each checkpoint is the fitness expected at its end.

Race paces use Daniels' race-equivalence tables. Threshold runs from halfway between 10K and half marathon pace out to half marathon pace. Steady is 8–13% slower than marathon pace and easy is 20–32% slower. The timeline check is a rule of thumb: up to about 0.3% faster per week is realistic for most trained runners, and over 0.5% is aggressive.
